06

Straight answers.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. Everything is month to month. Give us ten days’ notice before your paid-through date and you’re done — no termination fee, no “we need it in writing at the corporate office.”

Is there an admin fee?

No. Your first payment is a prorated month, plus protection if you choose it, plus a lock if you need one. That is the entire list, and the calculator above shows it to the cent before you leave the house.

What do I need to bring to move in?

A driver’s licence and a card. If you reserved online, your gate code is active by the time you park. If you didn’t, it takes about six minutes at the counter.

Can I get in after hours?

The main gate runs 6:00a to 10:00p, seven days. Rows A and B are wired for true 24-hour access — add it to any unit in those rows for $10 a month and your code stops caring what time it is.

Do I actually need climate control here?

In Washington County, a closed metal unit runs well past 110° in July. If you’re storing electronics, leather, vinyl records, candles, photographs, musical instruments or solid-wood furniture, take Building E. Patio furniture, tools and tubs of Christmas decorations are fine outside.

What can’t I store?

Anything alive, anything perishable, anything flammable, and anything illegal. Drain the fuel and disconnect the battery on mowers and generators. Tyres are fine; fifty-five gallons of diesel is not.

Is my property insured?

Not by us automatically — that’s true of every facility, whatever the sign says. Many homeowner and renter policies already extend to off-site storage, so check yours first. If it doesn’t, our protection plans start at $11 a month.

What if I picked the wrong size?

Move up or down for free inside the first thirty days, subject to what’s open on the board. After that it’s a normal transfer with the rate difference prorated.
