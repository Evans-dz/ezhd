The Course

Sculpted by fire.
Finished by a master.

A thousand years ago, lava poured across this desert and froze mid-motion. Eighteen emerald ribbons thread through it now, tee to green. The result is one of golf's most distinctive playing experiences: rugged, cinematic, and unforgettable.

The Lava Fields

A ribbon of emerald threaded between thousand-year-old basalt flows. The fairway narrows exactly where courage pays — pick your line off the tee and commit.

The Cauldron

  • Par 3
  • 187 yds

A single green ringed by black rock, nothing between you and it but carry. One swing, no compromise — the most photographed hole in Southern Utah.

Black Canyon

The canyon swallows the timid line. Flirt with the cliffs on the right and the reward is a short iron home. The hardest yes you'll say all day.
