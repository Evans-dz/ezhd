Inside the concept
What changes

A brand, not a template

Bespoke design system built from Entrada's own identity — the spiral sun, sandstone, lava, and light — instead of a shared club CMS theme.

Storytelling that sells

The Kidd redesign, the signature stretch, Sol Mesa at sunset — sequenced like a film, so prospects feel the club before they ever visit.

Real interactivity

Scroll-driven hole tours, a living routing map, tactile cards and cursors — engagement that keeps prospects exploring.

Mobile-first polish

Native swipe galleries, a persistent inquiry action, and type that holds its elegance on a phone screen.

Measurably faster

Self-hosted fonts, tuned images, zero template bloat — speed a member feels and Google rewards.

Accessible & findable

Semantic structure, keyboard support, reduced-motion respect, and clean metadata — modern standards, no plugins.
