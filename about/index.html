WhyWhy we started

Every business struggles with how it looks online. Not because the owner doesn’t care — because caring is expensive. Posts, photos, website changes: keeping up takes an owner 40 to 60 hours a month, on top of actually running the place. That’s a second job. It gets done at night, or it doesn’t get done.

We started EZHD to take that job. Two of us, based in St. George, working for businesses across Utah. You run the company. We make it known online.

HowHow we work

  • One accountable partner — Website, social, photography. One name answers for all of it.
  • A report every month — Written in plain English. The slow months go in the report too.
  • You set the pace — Steady, Active, or Full. Everything’s included at every plan — the pace is the only thing that changes.
  • Priced on a call — No published rates, because no two businesses need the same work. We quote after we’ve heard yours, and a build runs one to two weeks.

StraightWhat we’re not

  • Not a big agency — No account managers, no handoffs — the two people you meet are the two people doing the work.
  • Not a template shop — Every site is built by hand for the one business it belongs to.
  • Not a vendor stack — You won’t juggle seven logins and seven invoices. The parts live under one roof.
  • Not national — St. George is home; all of Utah is the territory.
