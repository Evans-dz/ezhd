Social media, kept alive.

Content and social media management for businesses in Logan, Smithfield, and across Cache Valley — shot on site, edited by us, and published on a schedule you never have to think about. Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Google Business Profile.

  • On-site capture sessions scheduled to your plan
  • Professional editing into reels, stories, and feed posts

We don't sell content by the piece. You pick a cadence — light, standard, or heavy — and the channels that fit your business, and we quote that on a call. The accounts, the footage, and the finished posts are yours from day one.

Why it failsThe problem is April, not January

Look up almost any small business in northern Utah and you'll find the same account: a burst of posts when someone got motivated, then months of nothing. A burst of posts in March, silence until July. Nobody planned it that way — the owner just ran out of hours, because posting was always the job that could wait until tomorrow.

That gap is worse than it looks. An account that's gone quiet tells a new customer the business might have too, and it tells the platforms to stop showing you to anyone. Consistency beats cleverness on every one of these channels, and consistency is exactly what a busy owner can't buy from their own calendar. So that's the part we take off your plate entirely — not advice, not a strategy PDF, the actual doing.

Being local is what makes the shooting part work. We're based in Cache Valley, so a capture session at your shop in Providence or a job site in Hyrum is a short drive, not a production. Real footage of your actual work, your crew, and your customers — because original content outperforms stock in every feed, and nobody has ever hired a contractor off a stock photo.

How it goesShoot, cut, schedule, answer

We start with a planning call: your channels, your cadence, and what next month needs to say. Then we come shoot — capture sessions are scheduled to your plan, batched so one visit feeds weeks of posts. We edit everything into reels, stories, and feed posts, write the captions and hashtags, and load it all into a monthly calendar you approve before anything goes out.

After that, it publishes on schedule whether your week got busy or not. On business days we watch the accounts — answering comments, messages, and reviews in your voice, and flagging anything that needs you personally. Once a month you get a plain-English report on what ran and what it did. No dashboard homework, no jargon.

QuestionsStraight answers

Do we need to be on every platform?
No, and we'll usually talk you out of it. A trades business in Cache Valley often does its best work on Instagram, Facebook, and Google Business Profile; a B2B company may only need LinkedIn. Fewer channels done consistently beats five done badly — we pick together on the planning call.
Will this get us followers or leads?
We won't promise a number — anyone guaranteeing you a number is guessing. What we do promise is the part you can actually control: professional content going out on schedule, every week, with someone answering when people respond. Our first clients run from St. George to right here in the valley on exactly that arrangement — Southern Utah shoots just take a little more scheduling.
What does it cost?
Quoted per project, based on the cadence and channels you choose. Pick your pieces with the scope builder and we price it on a twenty-minute call.
