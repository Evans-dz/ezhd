Why customTemplates look like templates

Most small-business sites in northern Utah come off the same three page builders, and it shows: the same hero, the same stock photos, the same layout your competitor bought last month. A custom build costs more attention up front, but it gives you the two things a template can't — a site that looks like your business instead of your industry, and clean, fast code that search engines actually favor.

Speed matters double out here. A contractor in Hyrum or a clinic in Providence isn't competing with the whole internet — they're competing with a dozen local rivals on the same searches. A fast, well-structured site with honest local content is often the whole difference on page one.

How it goesKickoff to live in one to two weeks

Day 0 is a discovery call — brand assets, goals, and content gathering. Days 1–6 we design and build. Days 7–9 you review, with two rounds of revisions applied. Days 10–14: final checks, domain connection, and launch. The schedule only moves when feedback does, and we tell you that on day one.
