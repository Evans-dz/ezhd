06 · THE MONTHLY REPORT

A REPORT EVERY MONTH.
THE SLOW ONES TOO.

The site going live is the start of the work, not the end of it. Every month you get a written report on what the site, the Google profile and the ads actually did, and what we did about it. It is written to be read, not to impress you.

What’s in it

  • What ran, across the site, the Google profile and the ads.
  • What we changed, and why we changed it.
  • What we’re doing next month.
  • What we don’t know yet, said plainly.

How we report

  • If a month produced no calls, messages or appointments, the report says so, and says whether that’s expected at this stage or a problem to fix.
  • If a number jumps, we say whether it jumped off a near-zero base. Growth like that is real, but it isn’t a repeatable monthly rate, and we won’t hand it to you as one.
  • If two platforms count the same thing differently, we show both as reported rather than quietly reconciling them into one tidier figure.
  • We tell you up front which numbers matter and which month to start judging them by. Month one is not that month.

Not shown here Client numbers. Those are theirs, not marketing material. Ask and we’ll walk you through the format with the figures taken out.
